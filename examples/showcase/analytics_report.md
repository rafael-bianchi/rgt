In Q3 2025 the company generated revenue of $337556 against operating costs of $230261, serving approximately 34,669 active users with a churn rate of 3.9%.
In Q4 2025 the company generated revenue of $400830 against operating costs of $248687, serving approximately 33,609 active users with a churn rate of 4.6%.
In Q1 2026 the company generated revenue of $295630 against operating costs of $182897, serving approximately 9,083 active users with a churn rate of 4.0%.
